Shipping & returns.

Every order is packed by us and ships by USPS with tracking. You get the tracking number by email when it leaves.

Rates and times

United States
USPS Ground Advantage, 2–5 business days. $6 for tees and accessories, $9 if your order has a hoodie or crewneck, plus $2 per extra piece.
International
USPS First-Class Package International, 7–21 business days. $15 for tees and accessories, $25 with a hoodie, plus $5 per extra piece.
Australia & NZ
$18 for tees and accessories, $30 with a hoodie, plus $5 per extra piece.

We ship to the US, Canada, Mexico, the EU, Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Hong Kong, Taiwan, the UAE and Israel. Pick your country in the bag to see the exact rate before you pay. UK shipping opens soon.

Duties and taxes

International parcels ship delivery-duties-unpaid. Your country may charge import VAT, duty or a handling fee when the parcel arrives; that’s paid to the carrier, not to us.

Lost & Found and location-exclusive pieces may be fulfilled differently — details come with each drop.

Returns

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed and in original packaging.

To start a return, email hello@onlyatthekiosk.com with your order number (KIOSK-…).
