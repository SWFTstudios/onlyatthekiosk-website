FAQ

Questions, answered.

Ordering, shipping, returns and how the kiosk works. Something missing? Email hello@onlyatthekiosk.com.

How do I place an order?

Pick a size, add it to your bag and check out. Payment runs through Stripe: major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship by USPS, with tracking, to the US, Canada, Mexico, the EU, Australia, New Zealand and much of Asia; pick your country in the bag to see the rate. Import duties and taxes, if any, are paid on delivery. UK shipping opens soon. Details on Shipping & returns.

How can I track my order?

You’ll get a tracking number by email the moment it ships, and it’s on your order page and in your account.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed and in original packaging. Email us with your order number to start one.

Are your products limited edition?

Most KIOSK drops are limited runs. Marketplace and Lost & Found pieces may be one-of-a-kind.

What is Lost & Found?

Location-based treasure-hunt drops. You receive coordinates to a physical place where you can unlock or claim an exclusive piece. See the map.

What is the KIOSK marketplace?

A curated space for independent brands, thrift stores and remote sellers. Some pieces ship online; others are only available at physical KIOSK locations. Browse the stores.

Do I need an account?

No — you can check out as a guest. A free account keeps your saves, check-in, bag and orders on every device. No password: we email you a sign-in code.
