About

Essentials that feel like discoveries.

KIOSK is part clothing brand, part marketplace, part travel experience.

Essential clothing for any occasion. KIOSK brings together the essentials vision of KIOSK and WLG into one discovery-driven brand.

What KIOSK is

We start with simple, testable products — chains, essential tees and wardrobe staples — then expand into hoodies, sweaters, pants, shirts, accessories and more.

As we grow, independent brands, thrift stores and remote sellers connect through KIOSK, creating a curated marketplace of unique pieces, rare finds and location-exclusive drops.

Built for everywhere

KIOSK is building toward a future where stores, vending machines and circular clothing racks exist at airports, near hotels, in cities and in remote places — giving people instant access to elevated wardrobe essentials wherever they are.

Categories

Essential tees, hoodies, sweaters, shirts, pants, accessories, chains, one-off pieces and rare finds. The shop focuses on chains, essential tees and hoodies today, with more categories arriving in future drops.

The marketplace

Some pieces ship online. Others are exclusive to physical KIOSK locations. The marketplace brings together independent brands, thrifted and curated finds, and location-only pieces under one roof. See the stores at the kiosk.

Lost & Found

Treasure-hunt drops: you get coordinates to a remote or unexpected place, travel there and claim an exclusive piece — shopping as a trip. Open the Lost & Found map.

Why KIOSK exists

To inspire people to travel, explore and keep coming back through a fun, interactive way to shop — and to give remote store owners, thrift stores and independent brands more exposure.
